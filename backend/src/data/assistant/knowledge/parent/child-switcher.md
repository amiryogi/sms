# Switching between your children

## Purpose
Parents with more than one child at the school see each child's information separately.

## Who can use it
Parents.

## Where to find it
At the top of every parent page: **Choose a child**.

## Steps
1. Open any page under **My children** (Dashboard, Attendance, Assignments, Results, Report Card,
   Fees, Child's Profile).
2. Use **Choose a child** at the top to pick the child.
3. The choice stays while you move between pages in this browser session.

## What you should see
The page shows only the chosen child's information.

## Common mistakes
- Expecting both children on one page: switch between them.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No children linked"**: ask the school office to link your child to your account.
- **"Not enrolled this year"**: the child is not enrolled in a class for the current year.
