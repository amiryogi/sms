# Fees

## Purpose
See what is due, what has been paid, receipts and any discount or scholarship.

## Who can use it
Students (their own) and parents (their linked children).

## Where to find it
Left menu → **Fees** (`/student/fees` or `/parent/fees`).

## Steps
1. Open **Fees**. Parents: choose the child at the top first.
2. See each fee and installment with its due date (**Was due** when it has passed) and status.
3. Under **Receipts**, use the print button (**Print receipt**) on a receipt.
4. **Print bill** prints a fee bill; the statement link prints the full statement.
5. **Discounts and scholarships** lists approved ones.

## What you should see
The balance for this year, and every payment with its receipt number.

## Common mistakes
- Looking for online payment: fees are paid at the school office; the app records and shows them.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No fees yet"**: the school has not set up fees for this year.
- A payment is missing or wrong: ask the school's accounts office. A **Reversal** entry means a
  payment was cancelled by the school.
