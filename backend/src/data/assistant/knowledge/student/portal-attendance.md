# Checking attendance

## Purpose
See this year's register day by day, by Nepali month.

## Who can use it
Students (their own) and parents (their linked children).

## Where to find it
Left menu → **Attendance** (`/student/attendance` or `/parent/attendance`).

## Steps
1. Open **Attendance**. Parents: choose the child at the top first.
2. Look at **Register by month**: each day the register was taken shows present, absent, late or
   excused. Hover or tap a day for details.
3. Move between Nepali months.

## What you should see
The days taken so far and the attendance rate.

## Common mistakes
- Expecting to change attendance: only teachers mark it. Ask the class teacher about a mistake.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No attendance yet"**: days appear once teachers take the register.
- Parents with the mobile app get a notification when their child is marked absent today.

## Related pages
