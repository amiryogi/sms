# Reading school notices

## Purpose
Announcements and updates from the school.

## Who can use it
Teachers, exam officers, accountants, students and parents read notices. Only administrators
write and publish them.

## Where to find it
Left menu → **Notices**.

## Steps
1. Open **Notices**.
2. Use **Search notices** or filter by **Priority**.
3. Open a notice to read it.

## What you should see
Published notices meant for you: for everyone, for your role, or for your (or your child's)
class.

## Common mistakes
- Looking for a button to create a notice: only administrators can.

## Troubleshooting
- A notice you expected is missing: it may still be a draft, it may not have started showing
  yet ("Show from"), it may have expired ("Show until"), or it is aimed at another class or role.
- With the mobile app installed you may also get a phone notification when a notice is published.
